## Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
Describe any world — a noir mystery in 1940s Chicago, a space station hiding dark secrets, a magical kingdom on the edge of war — and Cosmonaut builds it into a branching story shaped entirely by your choices.

✨ HOW IT WORKS

1. Describe your story — set the genre, the setting, the mood. You set the stage, AI fills in the details.
2. Every choice branches — decisions create new paths. Write custom actions, explore unexpected directions. Your story grows with every tap.
3. See the whole picture — a visual story map shows every path through your narrative. Jump between branches, discover what-ifs, find the endings you missed.

🚀 KEY FEATURES

• AI-Powered Storytelling — Describe any premise and watch a rich, branching narrative unfold in real time. Stories stream to your screen as they're written.

• Your Choices Matter — Every decision opens a new path. Choose from AI-generated options or write your own custom actions to take the story anywhere you imagine.

• Visual Story Map — See your entire story tree at a glance. Navigate between branches, track your exploration, and discover paths you haven't taken yet.

• Audio Narration — Listen to your stories with high-quality AI voices. Choose from multiple narrators, adjust playback speed, and enjoy stories hands-free.

• Share & Explore Together — Share your story worlds with friends and family. Create invite links, explore together, and see how others navigate the same story differently.

• Beautiful Dark Theme — Designed for immersive reading with a premium space-themed interface that looks stunning on any device.

• Content Controls — Choose vocabulary level and content filters to make stories appropriate for the whole family. Child, teen, and adult settings available.

📖 BUILT FOR FAMILIES

Cosmonaut is designed to be enjoyed by readers of all ages. Set content filters and vocabulary levels to create age-appropriate adventures. Create stories together — each family member can explore different branches of the same world.

🎧 LISTEN ANYWHERE

Generate high-quality audio narration for any story passage. Multiple AI voices to choose from, lock screen controls, and background playback — perfect for bedtime stories or on-the-go adventures.

🌟 FREE TO START

Create stories and explore branching narratives with a free account. Upgrade to Explorer or Cosmonaut tiers for more worlds, more choices, and expanded features.

Cosmonaut — where every choice creates a new world.
```

## Data Safety Section

### Data collected

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Email address | Yes | No | Account management, authentication |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Account functionality |
| Name (username) | Yes | No | In-app display, story sharing |
| App interactions | Yes | No | Analytics, product improvement |
| Crash logs | Yes | No | App stability, bug fixing |
| App performance | Yes | No | Performance monitoring |

### Security practices

- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Data is encrypted at rest (AWS infrastructure)
- Users can request data deletion (Settings → Delete Account)
- Data is not sold to third parties

### Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| AWS Cognito | Authentication | Email, password hash |
| Sentry | Error tracking | Crash logs, device info |
| PostHog | Product analytics | Anonymous usage events |

| Stripe | Subscription billing | Handled externally via web |
